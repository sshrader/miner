Read this first. This is a game, not an investment, a job or a promise of income. Rewards come from a small pool that someone chose to put up. They can run out, be paused or be removed at any time, and you can get nothing. Nothing here is financial advice. Play only if it is lawful where you live.

How it works

  • Your miner digs a shared mine. Rewards are hidden inside blocks; whoever's miner breaks the right block is paid, straight to the wallet address you entered.
  • Every reward on a level must be found before the next level opens.
  • We only ever need your public address. We never ask for a seed phrase or private key, and no one from this game will ever ask you for one.

Fair play limits

    What can happen

    • Payouts can be paused (by the admin, by an hourly safety cap, or automatically if payments keep failing). Digging continues; rewards stay hidden until payouts resume.
    • Payments are on-chain and final. A payment sent to the wrong address cannot be taken back, so check your address.
    • Wallets that look like they are cheating (many addresses from one place, bought-and-sold holdings, bots) can be removed or muted without notice.
    • When the season ends, anything left in the pool is returned to the pool owner.
    • The game may be changed, reset or shut down at any time.

    Chat

    Be decent. No links, spam or harassment. Chat can be muted.
