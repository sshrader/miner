How it works

  1. Send a miner inConnect Phantom, or just paste your wallet address. Pasting only reads your public address, so nothing can be signed or spent. Your miner rides the elevator down into the shared mine. Just curious? Choose “Just watch” and see the mine without any wallet.
  2. It digs by itselfYou don't steer. Every miner works the same cave in real time, and you can watch from any angle. Blocks get tougher as the mine gets deeper.
  3. Rewards are hidden in the wallsEach reward sits inside a block. When a miner breaks the right one, the game pays the wallet that miner belongs to, on-chain, right then. Others see it happen live.
  4. Level by levelEvery reward on a level has to be found before the next level opens, so nobody can skip straight to the bottom. Miners that finish a level wait at the elevator for the next one.
  5. Holding rule
  6. Room for everyone
  7. The pool refillsWhen more funds arrive in the reward wallet, they turn into new hidden rewards. If the pool runs dry, there is nothing left to find until it is topped up.
Good to know. This is a game, not an investment or a job, and finding nothing is a normal outcome. Read the rules and the FAQ.
